Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how we compile mandi rates? Check details below

How do I get mandi prices from GramIQ?
You can get daily rates by checking this site, subscribing to our RSS feeds, or subscribing directly via WhatsApp to receive automatic notifications whenever rates are updated.
Where does this price data come from?
All prices are sourced from data.gov.in (the Open Government Data Portal of India), which aggregates official reports directly submitted by APMC mandis across India.
Are these prices official buy rates?
No, these rates are indicative average modal rates for the day. Local grading, quality parameters, moisture content, and market conditions will influence final quotes. Always verify locally before transaction.
